# Kenney model assets

The `.glb` files in this directory are from Kenney's **Nature Kit 2.1** and **Fantasy Town Kit 2.0**. Both are distributed under CC0; the original license texts are in this directory. Credit: [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/).

To make the upstream Bridge example reproducible without downloading the full packs, only the 26 models named by its loader and their shared `Textures/colormap.png` are included:

- Nature Kit: [mirror, pinned revision `182ed853`](https://github.com/BastiaanOlij/godot-vr-weapons/tree/182ed8535aabbbd1233ef700d32952fcd3b98613/assets/kenney.nl/naturekit).
- Fantasy Town Kit: [mirror, pinned revision `a620da71`](https://github.com/jeremyaboyd/larp-village/tree/a620da71d842dfcf5b0cb9a69c9aa97b98bb93e4/godot/assets/kenney/town).

These are third-party assets, separate from the upstream skill's MIT-licensed code and from its private style-study frames.
